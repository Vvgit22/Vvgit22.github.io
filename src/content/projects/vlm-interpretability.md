## Problem

Multimodal vision-language models can read a scan and a report together and propose a clinical hypothesis. Before such a proposal can be trusted in oncology, it has to be clear what in the image and the text the model relied on, and whether that evidence would convince a clinician.

## The project

A confirmed research project with Prof Guang Yang's group at Imperial, starting in the 2026–27 academic year, on interpretability methods for multimodal vision-language models used for clinical hypothesis generation in oncology. Methods, data and results will appear here as the work progresses.
